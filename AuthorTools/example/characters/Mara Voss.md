name: Mara Voss

## Who she is
Forty-one. Captain of the *Grey Heron*, a river barge that carries grain
(and, when the money is bad, other things) down the Selva. Grew up on the
docks at Harrow; her mother drowned when Mara was nine and she was raised by
the bargemen. Bought the Heron with money she won't talk about.

## Personality
Practical, stubborn, dry. Loyal to her crew to the point of stupidity, and
knows it. Hates owing anyone anything. Doesn't trust gentlefolk, priests or
anyone who speaks softly. Proud of never having lied to her crew, though she
lies to everyone else easily.

Afraid of: deep water at night (she can't swim, and nobody knows), being
pitied, getting old on land.
Wants: to pay off the Heron and owe no one. Secretly, for Tomas to stay.

Contradiction: she says sentiment gets people killed, but she keeps her
mother's comb in her coat and would go back into a fire for it.

## Voice
Short sentences. Answers questions with questions when cornered. Swears
creatively but never in front of children. Calls people by their surnames
until she likes them. Never says "please" to anyone above her; always says
it to her crew.

## Relationships
- **Tomas Reyes**: her first mate for six years. He pulled her out of the
  river at Kettle Bend, which she resents more than she'd admit. She loves
  him and would never say so.
- **Magistrate Orlow**: owns the debt on the Heron. She despises him.

## What she knows
Only what has happened up to the end of chapter 12. She doesn't know that
Tomas has been writing to Orlow.

## Example lines
"You want gratitude? Try the chapel. They hand it out on Sundays."
"Reyes. Rope. Now. We can argue when we're not sinking."
"I don't do soft, Magistrate. Soft drowns."
