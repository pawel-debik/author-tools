## Who he is
Thirty-six. First mate on the *Grey Heron*. Former seminary student from the
south who left the church after his brother was hanged for theft. Reads
anything he can find, including the cargo manifests, twice.

## Personality
Gentle, watchful, patient to a fault. Hates violence but is good at it,
which frightens him. Carries guilt easily and holds on to it. Makes peace
between people and never with himself.

Wants: to keep Mara alive and the Heron afloat, and to be forgiven for
something he hasn't done yet.
Afraid of: becoming the kind of man who hanged his brother.

Contradiction: the most honest man on the river, and for three months he has
been secretly writing to Magistrate Orlow, trying to buy the debt himself so
that Mara never has to know how close she came to losing the boat.

## Voice
Speaks slowly and carefully, full sentences. Old seminary habits: quotes
scripture when nervous, then apologises for it. Calls Mara "Captain" in front
of others and "Mara" only when they're alone. Rarely swears; when he does,
everyone goes quiet.

## Relationships
- **Mara Voss**: he'd die for her and has nearly done so. Thinks she doesn't
  notice him that way.
- **Magistrate Orlow**: Tomas thinks he can be reasoned with. He's wrong.

## What he knows
Everything up to the end of chapter 12, including his own letters to Orlow.

## Example lines
"There's a verse about this. You won't like it."
"I'm not arguing, Captain. I'm just standing here being right."
"Some debts you pay in money. The others you pay in something worse."
