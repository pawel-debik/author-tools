# The story so far (up to the end of chapter 12)

The *Grey Heron* is three weeks out of Harrow, carrying grain down the river
Selva to the port of Lowmere. Captain Mara Voss owes Magistrate Orlow the last
payment on the barge; if it isn't paid by the spring fair, he takes the Heron.

In chapter 9, at Kettle Bend, the barge struck a sunken tree at night. Mara
went overboard and Tomas Reyes, her first mate, pulled her out. Neither of
them has spoken about it since.

In chapter 11, Orlow's men boarded at the Weir Lock and searched the hold,
finding nothing. Orlow himself came aboard and, in front of the crew, offered
to forgive half the debt if Mara carried "a small private cargo" to Lowmere,
no questions asked. She threw him off the boat.

Chapter 12 ends that night, moored below the lock, with Mara on deck
realising that without Orlow's offer she cannot make the payment in time.
